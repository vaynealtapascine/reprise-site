# Font sources

Both fonts are self-hosted and licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
The license texts are included in this directory.

- Bricolage Grotesque by Mathieu Triay:
  https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/bricolagegrotesque
- Literata by TypeTogether:
  https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/literata

Downloaded 2026-10-04. The variable font sources were subset to Latin characters
and typographic punctuation and converted to WOFF2 with FontTools.
Bricolage Grotesque retains its weight axis, with optical size 48 and width 100.
Literata Italic is instantiated at optical size 60 and weight 500.
